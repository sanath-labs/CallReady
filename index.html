      About CallReady

      CallReady is a web-based network performance analysis tool built as a Data Communication and Networking (DCN) mini-project. It checks connectivity, latency, jitter and download speed, then explains what those results mean for everyday online activities.

      Terms explained

      Latency

      How long your internet takes to respond. Lower is better for live conversations.

      Jitter

      How much your response time changes from one request to the next. Steady is better.

      Download speed

      How quickly your device receives information, measured in Mbps.

      Upload speed

      How quickly your device sends information. Important for your camera and microphone.
